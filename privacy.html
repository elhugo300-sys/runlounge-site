Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

RunLounge, San Antonio, Texas ("we", "us") is a social fitness app for run clubs. This policy explains what information the RunLounge app collects, how it is used and the choices you have. If you have questions, email elhugo300@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your sign-in details and profile (such as your name and email address), handled through Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you create. Posts, comments, likes, photos you choose to upload, event RSVPs, direct messages and the runs and workouts you log.
  • Health and fitness data. If you give permission, RunLounge reads workouts and related activity data (such as steps, distance, calories burned, exercise sessions and heart rate) from Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android so you can import runs and keep your personal records accurate. You choose which categories to allow and can change this at any time in your device's settings.
  • Location. If you give permission, the app uses your device location to show maps and nearby run information. You can turn this off in your device's settings.
  • Device and usage data. Basic technical data needed to run the service, such as app version, device type and notification settings.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign you in, show your feed, events, stats and messages.
  • To import and display your workouts and personal records.
  • To send notifications you have turned on, such as reminders.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

Who can see your information

Posts, comments, photos and event activity are visible to other signed-in RunLounge users. Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation and to app administrators when needed for safety or support. Health data you import is used to power your own stats and is not shown to others unless you choose to post it.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) to store data and run the app. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email elhugo300@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will process the request.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so users can only change their own content. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

RunLounge is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above shows the latest version.
